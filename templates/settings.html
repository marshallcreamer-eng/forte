How tone adapts per channel
Facebook
Warm, community storytelling. 100–200 words. 1–2 emojis. End with a parent question or call to action.
Instagram
Visual-first, punchy opening. 40–80 words. 3–5 emojis. 5–8 hashtags. Always #BeltonPrepAcademy.
LinkedIn
Professional but warm. Student outcomes and school mission focus. 100–200 words. No casual slang.
X / Twitter
One punchy sentence or stat. Max 260 characters. 1–2 hashtags. Optional emoji.
ClassDojo
Direct parent-facing announcement. 50–120 words. No hashtags. Plain English. End with any action needed.
Newsletter
Parent email paragraph. 80–150 words. Clear headline, key details, warm close. Ready for Mailchimp or ClassDojo story.
